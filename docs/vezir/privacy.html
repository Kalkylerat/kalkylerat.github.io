Vezir – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Vezir is a private personal assistant used by a single person, its owner. It has no other users and is not offered to the public. This policy describes how Vezir handles Google account data.

Whose data

Vezir accesses only the Google account of its owner, after he grants consent on Google's consent screen. No data from any other person's account is requested or collected.

What is accessed and why

  • Gmail: reading messages to notify the owner about school messages, receipts and invoices; creating drafts; moving advertising mail to the trash or archive and adding labels after the owner's approval. Vezir does not send mail and does not delete mail permanently.
  • Google Calendar: reading events to show the owner his day; creating, changing or removing events after his approval.
  • Google Drive: saving the owner's own invoice PDFs to a folder he chose.

Where data is kept

Vezir runs on a private server controlled by the owner. It keeps only what it needs to work: the access token, short records of processed messages, and a log of the changes it made so that they can be undone. Message text is sent to an AI model provider (Anthropic) only to classify or summarise it for the owner; it is not used for advertising.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, not shared with third parties and not transferred to anyone, except the processing described above that is necessary to provide the assistant's functions to its owner.

Limited use

Vezir's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored data can be deleted by removing it from the private server.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the support email address shown on the app's Google consent screen.
