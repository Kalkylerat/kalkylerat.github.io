Kalkylerat

Football match probabilities, every day, on X (@kalkylerat).

Each match card shows the market's chance for every outcome (bookmaker margin removed) next to the teams' own stats from the same competition. These are probabilities, not picks.

After the final whistle we post what happened and the chance it had before kick-off. Every Monday a report card compares last week's chances with what actually happened, misses included.
