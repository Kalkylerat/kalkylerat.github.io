Vezir

A private, single-user personal assistant.

Vezir is a personal assistant that its owner talks to through a private Telegram chat. It is built and used by one person for his own everyday organisation. It is not a public service and has no other users.

What it does

  • Reads the owner's own Gmail inbox to notify him about school messages, receipts and invoices.
  • Keeps the inbox tidy: moves advertising mail to the trash or archive after the owner's approval. It never deletes mail permanently and never sends mail.
  • Shows the owner's Google Calendar and adds, moves or removes events after his approval.
  • Saves the owner's own invoice PDFs to a folder in his Google Drive and prepares mail drafts.

Google account access

Vezir connects only to its owner's Google account, with his explicit consent. How that data is handled is described in the privacy policy.
